# Phase 8 — Referrals, Commissions and Boosts

## Referrals
Implement codes/links, attribution rules/windows, eligibility, self-referral prevention, duplicate/abuse prevention, reversals and dispute handling.

## Commissions
Rules must be versioned, effective-dated and calculated server-side with explicit amount, currency and rounding rules. Record calculation inputs, rule version and transaction reference. Support pending, payable, reversed and paid states. Never reward failed or unsettled transactions.

## Boosts
Allow eligible jobs, services or profiles to be boosted. Record start/end, placement and status. Activate only after verified payment. Clearly disclose paid placement and never promise guaranteed outcomes.

## Abuse controls and tests
Apply rate limits, anomaly detection and manual review where appropriate. Test attribution, self-referral, duplicate events, reversals, commission calculations/versioning and activation only after verified payment.

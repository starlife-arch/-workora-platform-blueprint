# Phase 4 — Jobs, Services and Applications

## Goal
Create the marketplace for employment opportunities, gigs and worker services.

## Employer/client listings
Support title, description, category, required skills, location, remote/on-site status, employment/work type, schedule, compensation/currency, openings, deadline and listing status. Distinguish full-time, part-time, contract, gig, casual/day work and service requests where appropriate.

## Worker services
Allow workers to publish service listings with clear descriptions, skills, pricing and availability.

## Search
Provide indexed, paginated search by category, location, work type, compensation, skills, remote status and freshness.

## Applications
Support cover message, CV/portfolio references and states: submitted, shortlisted, rejected, withdrawn and hired. Keep application history. Prevent duplicate applications and applications to closed/expired listings server-side.

## Lifecycle and safety
Listings support draft, published, paused, closed and expired states. Provide employer/worker dashboards and reporting for suspicious jobs/employers. Protect private CV access.

## Do not
Add escrow, payroll, wallet, commission or payment settlement in this phase.

## Tests
Cover lifecycle transitions, authorization, search pagination, duplicate applications and expiry rules.
